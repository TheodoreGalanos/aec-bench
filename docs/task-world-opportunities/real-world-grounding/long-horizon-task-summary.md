# Long-Horizon SSC Task Summary

This is the current roll-up for the long-horizon SSC work. It summarizes the design notes, one-template-per-note runnable stream, and all-product runnable expansion without adding a new evidence claim.

## Status Snapshot

- Design coverage: 20 SSC notes exist, made up of 19 physical/product clusters plus `SSC-20` as a regional standards and authority overlay.
- Design-product coverage: each note defines eight long-horizon product families, so the design pack contains 160 candidate task products.
- Runnable baseline coverage: 19/19 physical/product SSC notes have a first runnable template or package-contract example. `SSC-20` is excluded because it is not a physical/product cluster.
- All-product runnable coverage: all 19 physical/product SSC notes have runnable synthetic examples for all eight product families, giving 152 runnable physical/product packages.
- Domain-practice grounding: 19/20 SSC notes have web-backed practice notes. `SSC-20` remains the only design note without that layer.
- Main remaining material work: add `SSC-20` domain-practice grounding, then choose source-pack hardening targets.

## What Done Means Here

For the all-product stream, "done" means the physical/product SSC note has eight task-owned synthetic package templates recorded in `product-expansion-catalogue.md`, with focused tests and generated golden verifier evidence. The older one-template stream remains recorded in `runnable-template-catalogue.md`.

This does not claim accepted project evidence, authority approval, executable real source-pack parsing, generated benchmark readiness, or benchmark readiness. The next quality jump is source-pack hardening: concrete manifests, parser checks, source-value echo checks, provenance checks, negative cases, and clear evidence boundaries.

## Overlay SSC

`SSC-20` is the regional standards, authority, and review packet overlay. It has eight design-level long-horizon products but no physical/product runnable-template target in the all-product stream:

- regional standards overlay for a design package;
- permit and submission completeness;
- authority conflict and repair;
- source-policy and redrawn-fixture boundary;
- regional variant matrix for a physical product;
- review comment ledger and design response;
- standards edition drift and regression;
- acceptance evidence and non-claim boundary.

The immediate remaining design-note pass is to add the same compact domain-practice grounding layer used for the other notes.

## Portfolio Shape

The created tasks form five strong cross-cluster families:

- Road, drainage, ITS, and energy resilience: `SSC-01`, `SSC-03`, `SSC-13`, `SSC-17`, with `SSC-05` for power interfaces.
- Ground, support, and electrical safety: `SSC-07`, `SSC-14`, `SSC-05`, with `SSC-20` as the authority overlay.
- Process, equipment, controls, acoustics, and energy: `SSC-10`, `SSC-06`, `SSC-18`, `SSC-12`, `SSC-17`.
- Fire, hazard, building operations, and emergency modes: `SSC-19`, `SSC-08`, `SSC-05`, `SSC-17`, with `SSC-20` for review gates.
- Envelope, coastal, weather-exposed assets, and structural interfaces: `SSC-09`, `SSC-04`, `SSC-14`, `SSC-17`, with `SSC-20` for regional criteria.
