# Wind Facade Structural Package Gaps

## Source Gaps

- Clause-level wind pressure procedures are gated in ASCE 7, EN 1991-1-4, and AS/NZS 1170.2.
- Need public facade engineering calculation examples, ideally including elevation mark-ups, bracket reactions, and anchor checks.
- Manufacturer product metadata, a BBA certificate, bracket design resistance tables, and HRD-H anchor data-sheet evidence are available through Hilti. EJOT/CROSSFIX now adds independent non-Hilti product/approval evidence, including ETA documents for the subframe kit and wall-bracket fastening screws. Leviat/HALFEN HCW now adds manufacturer specification/intake forms for edge-of-slab, top-of-slab, and punched-window curtain-wall anchoring, plus a top-of-slab working-load datum.
- The remaining source risk is not pure single-vendor dependence. It is lack of redistributable issued project calculation packages and lack of clean public capacity tables from every product-builder/vendor route.
- Eurofox now reduces the generic worked-calculation shape gap: the public catalogue includes a sample static-analysis sheet with input data, wind load, support system, profile geometry, sliding-point checks, anchor-point checks, bolt/rivet/dowel checks, moment/deflection diagrams, stress ratio, and utilization percentages. Hilti Ireland, Williaam Cox/Rainscreen Works, and the PBC Today/SFS article also corroborate service outputs such as static calculations, elevation drawings, setting-out information, component/material lists, downloadable PDFs, NBS specification, budget costs, and fixings lists. This still does not provide an issued real-project report, real elevation, selected bracket schedule, anchor schedule, or final governing utilization package.
- WPS now reduces the public system-calculation gap: its exterior wall cladding panel system calculations expose CBC/IBC/ASCE wind, seismic, and gravity basis, connection spacing, Zone A/C tributary areas, panel dead loads, adjustable bracket/rail/clip components, backing variants, connection summary capacities, material properties, and screw allowable load tables. This still does not provide an issued real-project report, real elevation, selected project bracket/anchor schedule, or final governing utilization package.
- Knight/KWS now reduces the North American submittal and anchor-source-pack gap: public KWS routes expose MFI/THERMABRACKET product fields, guide details/specifications/data sheets, project-specific engineering availability, Professional Engineer project-specific spacing calculations, per-project maximum spacing/dead-load logic tied to live/dead/tension/pry/shear wall-anchor checks, ANKIT ultimate pull-out/tension values, embedment/spacing/edge-distance fields, and real project/product-selection context. This still does not provide a public issued KWS project engineering package, pressure-zone elevation, selected bracket/anchor schedule, or final governing utilization table.
- Ancon/Leviat now reduces an adjacent masonry-support source-pack gap: public MDC/Optima pages, a BS EN 1993 design sheet, Optima typical layout drawings, installation guides, and masonry-support/lintels literature expose cavity/load/fixing intake fields, Optima load/cavity tables, fixing-type layout drawings, MDC/stone/cleat specification syntax, shim/torque/tolerance rules, and project-layout drawing references. This still does not provide a completed rainscreen wind calculation, real pressure-zone elevation, filled manufacturer engineering response, selected project bracket/anchor schedule, or final governing utilization package.
- SFS/NVELOPE now has public sitemap, product, system, and resource-download evidence, plus brochures documenting Project Builder output fields, drawing-overlay service, fixed/sliding points, fixing levels, deterministic bracket option tables with size/zone-depth/product-code fields, a limited NH3 load chart, installation-guide evidence for static-calculation-driven bracket positions/fixing locations, rendered NV1 detail drawings for generic section/window/corner/cladding-zone conditions, rendered NV3 detail drawings for a generic panel-module cladding elevation and concealed-fix section/detail conditions, rendered NH3 system drawings for horizontal-support adjustment/detail-location/fixed-sliding guidance, SDA5 rail-fixing product and installation semantics, public NV/NH3/NVF2F BBA certificates with bracket design-resistance tables, rail/profile geometry, wind-action basis, pull-out-test responsibility, and combined vertical/horizontal utilization rules, and public case-study evidence for real Project Builder use, wind-load calculation/pricing statements, detailed design, cladding areas, panel mass, wall deviation, bracket ranges, extra brackets, custom rails/clips, and test/performance context. It still does not expose a filled Project Builder PDF, project drawing overlay, real project elevation, completed static-calculation report, selected bracket-centre schedule, anchor check, or project-specific utilization table through the inspected public routes.
- Current NVELOPE Australia and SFS UK Project Builder routes reduce the live intake/report-boundary gap by exposing calculator fields, drawing expectations, report contents, and login/register boundaries, but they still do not expose filled Project Builder exports, project drawing overlays, selected bracket/rail centres, static calculation excerpts, anchor checks, or utilizations. Hilti PROFIS reduces the generic anchor-report-shape gap with load-combination, reaction, tension, shear, combined-utilization, and status tables, but the public report is not facade-specific or tied to a rainscreen drawing/schedule.
- Hohmann & Barnard now reduces another North American source-pack gap: public EZclad/FLEXbracket pages and the HB Rainscreen Brochure expose project-conditioned engineering, engineering calculations and shop drawings as a service/output boundary, panel-size/type calculation dependency, vertical/horizontal bracket orientation, and load-table fields for wind/dead load, bracket spacing, wind-load demand, steel-stud screw pull-out, Fu/Fy, gauge, and pass rows. This still does not expose a returned project-specific H&B engineering calculation, real elevation, pressure-zone schedule, selected bracket/anchor schedule, or governing utilization.
- Cascadia Clip now adds public North American calculator/load-table evidence, including RDH structural design briefs, IAPMO UES spacer loads, fastener mechanical data, girt/hat-track section fields, a rendered assembly layout guide with clip spacing, rail orientation, substrate installation notes, embedment notes, and installation sequence, and direct calculator implementation evidence for option codes, endpoint query order, R/U/max-wind result mapping, chart arrays, and unsupported-state sentinels. The current product/quote/detail pass also adds download inventory, 3-part specifications, installation/detail/BIM routes, Jotform quote fields, area-by-area cladding schedules, clip/fastener/steel selections, adjustment shim/bracket options, and generic Z-girt/hat-track AutoCAD detail sets. It still does not expose a formal calculator export, the accompanying spreadsheet, a project-specific submittal, a real facade elevation, selected clip layout, or a bracket/anchor utilization chain.
- Fixfast, Downer Designer/download centre, BICEP, and Clad-Sec now add public calculator/request, framing-designer, project-engineering, and site-checklist routes. Fixfast is stronger after the SPIDI Max/MechFix brochure pass: it exposes bracket length/material/product-code fields, bracket-to-rail fastener fields, fixed/sliding/drillscrew/anchor location diagrams, substrate-specific component schedules, static/dynamic calculation wording, schematic/assembly drawing outputs, and materials/pricing schedule shape. The broader source family exposes useful fields for uploaded elevations, project wind loads, cladding weight/area, panel thickness/weight, fixing centres, rail orientation/configuration, bracket material, cladding zone, cavity/framing-zone depth, rail length/cantilever, substrate, static calculations, maximum rail lengths, peak fixing pull-out/shear advice, optimized component/fixing configurations, setout drawings, fixed/sliding point roles, bracket centres, and primary fixings. They still do not expose filled public reports or governing utilization checks.
- Targeted searches for filled Project Builder/static-calculation outputs, Downer/Fixfast examples, H&B/KWS returned packages, planning attachments, and facade-tied PROFIS reports did not recover a public returned-output packet. Downer project pages add real project/product-selection context for DCS031, including AC Hotel by Marriott and Broxbourne School, but still stop before returned calculations, elevations, setout drawings, selected fixing schedules, and utilization checks.
- The near-term benchmark path is therefore task-owned source-pack assembly rather than passive public-report discovery. The packet now has a task-owned redrawn facade elevation, embedded pressure schedule, selected support/anchor tables, anchor embedment/edge-distance/spacing checks, governing utilization table, annotated output contract, verifier-rule contract, first geometry oracle, docs-only variant matrix, row-level variant source overlays, expanded docs-only variant SVG/CSV files, and a docs-only verifier implementation brief, but it still needs executable verifier implementation, runtime packaging, and stronger public or authorized returned-output evidence before it can be called benchmark-ready.
- Need filled manufacturer intake forms, completed manufacturer engineering responses, actual selected bracket/fixing outputs, and utilization calculations. The HCW forms show what should be asked, not how a real project answered.
- Need filled Fixfast/Downer/BICEP/Clad-Sec-style request/output packets, including the uploaded/redrawn elevation or detail drawing, returned static calculation, maximum rail lengths, optimized cantilever, selected support system, setout drawing, bracket-centre schedule, primary-fixing schedule, peak pull-out/shear loads, product/material/pricing schedule, and any governing bracket/anchor utilization.
- Need clearer public evidence for eccentricity and bracket load-transfer assumptions beyond the extracted certificate/data-sheet fields.

## Data Gaps

- Real facade elevations are usually project-confidential.
- Public BIM/IFC examples rarely include full facade support systems.
- Wind-zone images are often in reports or calculations, not machine-readable datasets.
- Filled manufacturer questionnaires, vendor engineering responses, and bracket/anchor utilization sheets are usually project-specific and may be confidential.
