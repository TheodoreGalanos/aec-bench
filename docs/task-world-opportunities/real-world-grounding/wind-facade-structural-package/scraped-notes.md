# Wind Facade Structural Package Scraped Notes

## ASCE 7 Standard Page

- ASCE identifies ASCE/SEI 7-22 as the current "Minimum Design Loads and Associated Criteria for Buildings and Other Structures" page and links the ASCE Hazard Tool, wind-load training, errata, supplements, and related products.
- The public page is useful for authority metadata and available digital tools, but not for clause-level wind-pressure equations.

## Eurocode 1 JRC Page

- EN 1991 covers actions normally considered in the design of buildings and other civil engineering works.
- The official parts list includes EN 1991-1-4 for wind actions.
- This supports the regional chain from action basis to cladding load cases, but detailed pressure coefficients require the standard or national annexes.

## Eurocode 3 JRC Page

- EN 1993 covers steel design for buildings and other civil engineering works, including resistance, serviceability, durability, fire resistance, and bolted/welded joints.
- This is relevant to bracket and fixing checks once wind pressures are converted into support reactions.

## Facade Workflow Orientation

- Public curtain-wall technical summaries describe curtain walls as non-structural exterior walls that transfer lateral wind loads back to the main structure through anchors.
- The same summaries identify dead load, wind load, seismic movement, thermal movement, differential movement, and deflection limits as facade design concerns.
- The summaries also identify anchor spacing/deflection logic: facade members span between anchor points, and deflection limits are often expressed relative to span.
- Curtain-wall anchors must allow differential movement between the building structure and facade system, so a realistic fixing task is not only a strength check.
- Public rainscreen summaries describe a double-wall cladding system with an outer cladding layer, a cavity for drainage/ventilation, and an inner layer or weather barrier that carries water/air-control functions.
- These summaries reinforce that a realistic facade task needs both structural load-transfer checks and envelope/detail evidence.
- This is useful orientation only; public facade calculation packages and manufacturer capacity data remain the needed evidence.

## Fixfast Facade Calculation And Request Evidence

- Fixfast's design-calculation article says facade calculations assess loads or forces on a building facade and help avoid support-structure or fastener failure and over-specification.
- The article lists substrate and wind-load calculations to BS EN 1991-1-4, 3D U-value calculations, fire-rating tests, and onsite pull tests as common facade calculation categories.
- The same article identifies required calculation inputs: location, building plans, elevations, rainscreen area, substrate material and thickness, face panels, insulation, and preferred substructure bar profile.
- Fixfast maps cladding panels to wind pressure, fixed-point brackets to dead load plus lateral load from the profile above, and sliding-point brackets to thermal expansion plus lateral wind forces.

- Direct HTML capture of the Fixfast SPIDI Facade Calculation Request page exposed form fields for project name, postcode, building height, building plan/elevation upload, fallback fields when only one elevation exists and no drawing is available, AutoCAD file prompt, cladding type, cladding attachment, cladding weight, cladding support rails, cladding zone, and substrate.
