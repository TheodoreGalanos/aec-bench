## Research Interpretation

- The best candidates for near-term benchmark generation are stormwater, civil-ground retaining, PV production plus PV/BESS interconnection-source-pack variants, pump-station duty, and road geometry; stormwater is especially strong for executable model-fixture work now that Example 3 has a docs-only detention source-pack contract with hashes, model summary, expected output, verifier rules, negative cases, and verifier implementation brief. Its remaining model-fixture risk is controlled SWMM execution/report parsing rather than source discovery.
- The best candidates for multimodal evidence engineering are stormwater drawings/models, fire sprinkler submittal packets, facade elevations, retaining-wall cross-sections/boring plans/submittals, PV SLDs, arc-flash SLDs, road plan/profile and grade-crossing sheets, and rail plan/profile sheets.
- The highest standards-access risk remains fire protection, arc flash/earthing, facade wind/cladding, and railway signalling; fire is now stronger on AHJ submittal/review packet shape but still weak on licensed criteria, filled water-flow forms, and accepted worked calculations.
- The strongest meta-harness axis is regional reconfiguration: US/NFPA/ASCE/FHWA, EU/Eurocode/ERA, Australia/NZ/Austroads/ARR/AS/NZS, and owner/AHJ-specific overlays.
