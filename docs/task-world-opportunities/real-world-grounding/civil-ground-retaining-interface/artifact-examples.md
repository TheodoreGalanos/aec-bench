## Fixture Candidates

- Borehole log, lab results, groundwater readings, and interpreted soil parameter table.
- Retaining-wall section with retained height, slope, surcharge, drainage, and foundation level.
- Earth pressure diagram and stability check table.
- Interface memo tying civil geometry to geotechnical assumptions and drawing notes.
- WSDOT-style owner source pack:
  - Wall alignment plan/profile/section.
  - Boring/test-pit location plan, including maximum-height station and toe/backslope exploration.
  - Table of wall constraints: right-of-way, easement, adjacent assets, utilities, construction access, and environmental constraints.
  - Groundwater and drainage assumptions.
  - Checklist response for geometry, stratigraphy, groundwater, loads, limit states, and drawing completeness.
- Soil nail source pack:
  - Preliminary wall layout and design loads.
  - Geotechnical report excerpt with borings, observation pits, soil corrosion tests, strata, piezometric levels, and applied loads.
  - Critical cross-section, nail spacing/length/inclination, facing/drainage detail, and proof/verification test records.
  - Contractor design submittal checklist with calculation narrative, code references, load factors, seismic criteria, drainage evaluation, and signed drawings.
- Anchored wall source pack:
  - Soldier-beam/lagging or sheet-pile section, anchor layout, apparent earth pressure and surcharge diagram, anchor inclination, groundwater/drainage basis, and performance/proof test table.
  - Design-example style expected outputs: soldier-beam spacing/embedment, lagging thickness, passive resistance, anchor load, facing design load, and global stability factor.
