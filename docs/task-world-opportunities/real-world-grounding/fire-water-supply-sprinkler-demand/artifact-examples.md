## Fixture Candidates

- Hydrant flow test form with static, residual, pitot, outlet diameter/coefficient, date, hydrant IDs.
- Utility PFI statement plus field-test table with modelled residual pressures, measured pressure hydrant, measured flow hydrant, flow increments, and maximum allowed test flow.
- EPANET-style municipal supply model with hydrant/source nodes, reservoirs/tanks, pumps/valves, pressure constraints, and fire-flow demand cases.
- EPANET fire-flow node test with target junction, baseline demand, added fire demand, required pressure, emitter coefficient for available-flow method, and expected pressure/demand report fields.
- WNTR fire-flow impact packet with Net3 or another redistributable `.inp` network, PDD settings, fire-flow demand/time window, baseline pressure screen, scenario pressure results, impacted-junction count CSV, impacted-population CSV, and convergence notes.
- WNTR fire-flow oracle packet with the raw `fire_flow.py`, `fire_flow_tutorial.ipynb`, `Net3.inp`, `fire_flow_junctions_impacted.csv`, and `fire_flow_people_impacted.csv` files, pinned source URLs or commit, file sizes, line counts, SHA-256 hashes, license notices, `uv run --with wntr==1.4.0` reproduction command, 39-row expected-output tables, nonzero-location checks, aggregate totals, and exact CSV equality checks.
- Sprinkler plan/riser diagram with remote area, node references, pipe lengths, fittings, elevations, K-factors.
- Water supply curve and sprinkler demand curve intersection.
- Fire pump curve, available/operating/remaining-pressure summary, and pump/no-pump decision memo.
- AHJ submittal packet with stamped drawings/calculations, checklist fields, plan-review comments, water-flow request, water-supply graph, hydraulic data nameplate, product data sheets, backflow loss graph, fire pump curve, and inspection status.
- FM-style property-risk review packet with fire-protection drawings, sectional views, sprinkler details, material cut sheets, hydraulic calculations, actual hydrant-flow evidence, storage commodity/arrangement fields, seismic bracing evidence where applicable, pump curves, pump-room details, and underground fire-main scope.
- FM code-support storage-sprinkler packet with report excerpt, standard-change target, FM data-sheet reference where relevant, plan/elevation figure, commodity/storage table, sprinkler K-factor and pressure/flow/spacing fields, activation/damage results, gas/steel temperature criteria, recommendation boundary, and caveat flags separating research support from project approval.
- Canadian public-fire-flow worksheet with building construction/effective-area/occupancy/exposure fields, sprinkler-protection credit, residual pressure, duration, maximum-day demand, water-supply reliability, hydrant public/private recognition, and adequacy result.
- Canadian WS4 water-supply update packet with municipal/system/contact fields, hydrant spacing and coverage answers, inspection/teardown/flow-test frequencies, flow-test results attachment, hydrant map, engineering report, hydraulic-model fire-flow plus maximum-day-demand results, flow schematic, pressure zones, pump capacities, and dry-hydrant map.
- Residential 13D water-supply packet with house plan, riser detail, household water main/tap/meter data, backflow/meter pressure-loss information, stand-alone or multipurpose layout selection, local authority note, and water-duration basis.
- Regional branch worksheet that decides the authority path before calculation, such as NFPA 13/13R/13D, EN 12845/BS 9251, AS 2118.1/2118.4/2118.6, or FPAA101D/H.

## Remaining Artifact Need

- Public AHJ or utility hydrant-flow-test forms with real field names, preferably with filled or anonymized examples. Current AHJ checklist evidence is good, but filled water-flow forms remain missing.
- Curated benchmark-ready fire-flow model packets built from WNTR/EPANET-style networks with pinned expected outputs, scenario metadata, and provenance. WNTR now supplies a public model/procedure/output route, inspected expected-output tables, commit-pinned raw file URLs, a hash manifest, and a successful temporary WNTR 1.4.0 reproduction; the packet still needs task-sized curation, durable fixture placement, and should not be treated as accepted sprinkler-design evidence.
- AHJ-approved or standards-published sprinkler hydraulic calculation reports or worked examples.
- Public-view/licensed clause details and worked examples: NFPA, current EN 12845, AS 2118, AS 2419.1, current BS 9251, AHJ overlays.
- Specific FM data sheets, full data-sheet criteria, or worked FM-style project review examples. Public FM code-support reports now provide strong storage-sprinkler research evidence and selected report recommendations, but they do not replace full data-sheet access or filled project review artifacts.
- Filled FUS/municipal water-supply forms, WS4 attachments, or fire-flow worksheets with enough anonymized detail for benchmark fixtures.
