## Fixture Candidates

- Influent table with flow, BOD/COD, TSS, ammonia/TKN, alkalinity, temperature, and permit limits.
- Process flow diagram for extended aeration, SBR, or oxidation ditch.
- Reactor volume/MLSS/SRT/HRT/F:M table and oxygen/blower schedule.
- Diffused-air source pack with oxygen demand, transfer efficiency, alpha/beta, critical temperature, altitude, DO target, diffuser layout, and air requirement.
- Mechanical aeration source pack with certified transfer rate or task-provided transfer basis, motor power, redundancy, and largest-unit-out condition.
- Equipment schedule with blower/motor efficiency and operating hours.
- Energy audit/retrofit memo with baseline energy, blower/diffuser/control change, expected kWh or cost savings, and process constraint notes.

## Remaining Artifact Need

- Real process reports or design memoranda with calculation appendices tying influent loads, selected process type, reactor volume, oxygen demand, air/blower selection, power, RAS/WAS, and permit criteria together.
- Regional nutrient-removal permit examples and effluent criteria.
- Manufacturer blower/diffuser submittals or public equipment schedules with enough fields for power verification.
