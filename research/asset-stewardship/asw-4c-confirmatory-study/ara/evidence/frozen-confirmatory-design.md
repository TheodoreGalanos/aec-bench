# Frozen confirmatory design

ASW-4C carries forward the ASW-4A design without adaptation:

- 32 paired blocks and 64 trajectories;
- 16 H1 stable-inspected blocks;
- 16 H2 worsening-verification blocks;
- current station view and structured handover in each block;
- hidden windows of 86,400 and 115,200 simulated seconds;
- 16 model turns, 12 proposals, 32 host commands, and one fresh handover per trajectory;
- no temporal retrieval, external historical search, visible future schedule, or visible study window;
- binary required-follow-up continuity failure;
- structured handover minus current station view;
- meaningful absolute paired risk reduction of 0.25;
- 20,000 paired bootstrap samples with seed 20260729 and a two-sided 95 percent interval;
- at least 28 eligible pairs;
- host-fault arm imbalance no greater than two; and
- no replacement of missing pairs.

The report can conclude only supported, refuted, inconclusive, or
coverage-blocked under the frozen ASW-4A rules.
