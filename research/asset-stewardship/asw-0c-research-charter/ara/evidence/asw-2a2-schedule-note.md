# ASW-2A2 schedule evidence note

The first ASW-2A2 end-to-end test placed inspection after repair access became
available. The charter values make that order inconsistent:

- repair access becomes available at `L`;
- inspection takes `D`;
- follow-up breach occurs `D` after the due trigger; and
- obligation breach has event priority 3, while process completion has priority
  6.

An inspection started at `L` therefore completes at the breach time, and the
breach applies first. Revision `ASW-0C-2` starts and completes inspection before
the repair access window. This changes the schedule only. It does not change
physical condition, the package, action authority, trigger durations, or event
priority.
