At B5, “meaningful actions” means the physical predicate and consequence
needed by the future action:

- no-maintenance/deferral changes physical eligibility;
- capability can make restriction or transfer consequential;
- inspection changes evidence only;
- clearing and repair change only their physical targets; and
- verification generates new physical evidence.

B5 does not define who may act, action names, approval, work orders,
obligations, or closure. Those remain ASW-0C/ASW-1/ASW-2. A B5 AG-07 pass
cannot claim the institutional action system exists.

and package E2E. It proves the physical family is small and deterministic.

FMECA/schedule, actions/authority, obligations, continuity carriers, endpoint,
estimand, and budgets. ASW-0 cannot exit until both B5 and ASW-0C pass.
