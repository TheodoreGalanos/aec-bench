# Research continuation summary

The ASW-4 programme checkpoint accepts the first stewardship study and
authorizes ASW-5 only.

ASW-4 established a useful research object. The production world supported 64
planned model trajectories, retained typed world outcomes and host faults,
recovered exact hidden-endpoint states from durable history, and produced an
independently reloadable report.

The scientific result is bounded and negative. In the 28 analyzable matched
blocks, 16 pairs succeeded in both treatments and 12 failed in both. No pair
had a different binary outcome. The structured-handover minus current-view
risk difference was `0.00`, with a frozen two-sided 95 percent interval from
`0.00` to `0.00`. The frozen conclusion remains `inconclusive`; this checkpoint
does not change that label or claim a handover benefit.

## Checkpoint decision

- Accept ASW-4 as complete.
- Preserve the ASW-4C generation, outcomes, report, and conclusion unchanged.
- Treat the four host faults and two endpoint-prefix recoveries as retained
  execution evidence.
- Authorize provider-free ASW-5 design, implementation, and falsification.
- Keep ASW-6 through ASW-10 conditional.
- Keep the ASW-3C atomic-publication candidate local because no second
  task-world consumer exists.
- Require a new study freeze and separate provider authority before another
  model study.

## Next hypothesis

The ASW-5 hypothesis is provisional:

> Information continuity can affect outcomes or process quality when correct
> action depends on several overlapping required follow-ups, work processes,
> operating limits, and reserved resources.

ASW-5 tests the world semantics first. It must add composite triggers,
dependencies, waiver and suspension, nested operating limits, reservations,
spare and access processes, dependency-aware rescheduling and cancellation,
and overlapping work orders. These processes must replay and survive handover
without duplicated work, lost state, or resource-conservation errors.

The checkpoint does not assume that a richer world will make structured
handover better. It identifies a condition under which that question becomes
more demanding and measurable.
